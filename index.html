Flight Plan runs a hands-on aerospace and engineering program at the Monroe Township Library, welcoming kids in grades 1–5 and taught by dedicated high school volunteers.

See our programs

Why we exist

Most kids never meet an engineer before they decide engineering isn't for them.

Flight Plan puts real tools, real materials, and real engineers in front of kids early — so building things stops feeling out of reach. Every session is designed and taught by high schoolers passionate about aerospace and engineering, using the same design-build-test process used on the job.
